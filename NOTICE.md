# Data sources and credits

- **House roll call votes:** Office of the Clerk, U.S. House of Representatives (clerk.house.gov). Public record.
- **Senate roll call votes:** U.S. Senate (senate.gov). Public record.
- **Member roster:** [unitedstates/congress-legislators](https://github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators), public domain.
- **Bill summaries:** Congressional Research Service, via Congress.gov.
- **Seed data:** votes through 2026-11-12 were loaded from the open
  [Yeas & Nays](https://github.com/rachelsprague/yeas-and-nays) dataset, Copyright (c) 2026 Rachel Sprague,
  MIT License. The four XML files in `pipeline/fixtures/` are official records copied from that project's tests.
